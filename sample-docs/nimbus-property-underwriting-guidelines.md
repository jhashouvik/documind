# Nimbus Re - Property Facultative Underwriting Guidelines

Version 4.2 - effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Property Underwriting Committee.
Nimbus Re is a fictional reinsurer used for training material only.

## 1. Purpose and scope

These guidelines apply to all property facultative submissions written by the
Nimbus Re property team, including single risks, schedules of locations and
portfolio covers placed on a facultative basis. They set the minimum standards
for risk selection, pricing, documentation and referral. Deviations require
written approval from the Chief Underwriting Officer.

## 2. Underwriting authority

Authority is granted per underwriter grade and is expressed as the total
insured value (TIV) of the risk:

- Grade A underwriters may bind risks with a TIV up to USD 100 million.
- Grade B underwriters may bind risks with a TIV up to USD 25 million.
- Trainee underwriters have no binding authority; every quote needs a Grade A
  countersignature.

## 3. Referral requirements

Any risk with a total insured value above USD 250 million must be referred to
the Chief Underwriter before a quote is issued, regardless of the underwriter's
grade. The referral must include the latest risk survey, five years of loss
history and the proposed pricing model output.

Risks must also be referred when:

- the occupancy is on the restricted list (fireworks, bulk chemical storage,
  waste recycling, sawmills);
- the insured has had more than two losses above USD 1 million in the last
  five years;
- the requested line exceeds the maximum line size in section 4.

## 4. Capacity and line size

The maximum line Nimbus Re will write on a single risk is USD 75 million for
its own share. For risks in earthquake zone 4, the maximum share is limited to
25 percent of the order, even if the line size would allow more.

## 5. Construction and protection

Wood-frame buildings with more than four storeys are declined.

Warehouses larger than 5,000 square metres require a full automatic sprinkler
system designed to NFPA 13 or EN 12845, with a documented annual inspection.
Warehouses without adequate sprinklers may only be written at a 40 percent
rate loading and with a maximum line of USD 10 million.

High-rack storage above 12 metres requires in-rack sprinklers.

## 6. Natural catastrophe

### 6.1 Earthquake

For risks located in earthquake zone 4, the minimum deductible is 2 percent of
the total insured value, applied per occurrence. Buildings constructed before
1980 in zone 4 require a structural engineering report.

### 6.2 Flood

Locations inside a 100-year floodplain are subject to a flood sublimit of no
more than 10 percent of the TIV. Basements in a floodplain are excluded unless
flood barriers are installed and tested annually.

### 6.3 Windstorm

Coastal locations within 5 kilometres of the shoreline in named-storm regions
carry a windstorm deductible of 5 percent of the location value.

## 7. Risk surveys

A risk survey by an approved engineer is mandatory for every risk with a TIV
above USD 50 million. Surveys are valid for 36 months. Critical survey
recommendations must be completed within 90 days of binding, or the cover is
subject to a warranty.

## 8. Pricing

The minimum technical rate for commercial property is 0.05 percent (5 basis
points) of the TIV. A loss-free discount of up to 15 percent may be applied when
the insured has been loss-free for five consecutive years. Brokerage above 17.5
percent must be approved by the Head of Property.

## 9. Documentation

Every bound risk must have, in the underwriting file: the signed slip, the
survey report, the pricing model output, the referral approval (if any) and a
short underwriting rationale of no more than one page.
